GymChaos

A fitness arcade brawler in Unity where the whole world is one gym: you train, level up and fight the regulars.

Year
2026, in development
Built with
Unity, C#, Blender
My part
Design, code and most of the 3D assets

The idea

GymChaos takes place entirely inside a fitness building. You work out to grow your physique, interact with your gym mates and, when it goes wrong, fight them. The characters are modelled on popular bodybuilders and fitness icons.

What I built

  • Workout stations and a progression system with XP, mastery and reputation. There is no money and no shop.
  • Gym visitors who walk in through the door, train and leave, on a day and night cycle.
  • Enemies that roam the gym on a NavMesh and fight back.
  • A radio on the reception desk with its own playlist.
  • Most of the 3D assets, made in Blender. Some character models started in Microsoft Copilot Labs, an image-to-3D tool.

Result

The game runs in the browser as a WebGL build. Unity and WebGL do not get along on every feature, so the desktop build is the complete version.
